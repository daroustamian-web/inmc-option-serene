/ 01 What HBOT Is [ 01 ]

More oxygen, delivered under pressure.

Hyperbaric oxygen therapy (HBOT) raises the air pressure around you while you breathe oxygen. Under that pressure, more oxygen dissolves into your blood plasma and reaches tissue that normal breathing does not reach as easily.

You lie or sit comfortably in the chamber, fully clothed. Most patients read, rest, or sleep. You may feel your ears pop as pressure changes, the same way they do on a plane.

Dr. Santiago reviews your health history first and fits HBOT into your wider plan, often alongside IV nutrition, ozone, or PEMF therapy.

/ 03 What Research Has Explored [ 03 ]

Questions patients ask us about.

Researchers have studied hyperbaric oxygen for several conditions. Results are mixed and still developing. Dr. Santiago will tell you honestly whether HBOT makes sense for you.

Brain injury & cerebral palsy

A double-blind study of children with cerebral palsy (Hardy et al.) found that children in both the oxygen group and the pressurized-air group improved, with no clear difference between the two.

Autism & ADHD

A 2009 multicenter trial (Rossignol et al.) reported improvements after 40 one-hour sessions at 1.3 atmospheres. Later studies have not all agreed, and research continues.

Migraine

A small study of migraine with aura (Wilson et al.) found lower reported pain after hyperbaric oxygen than after the control treatment.

Recovery & wellness

Many patients add HBOT to an existing plan for recovery, energy, and general wellbeing. Dr. Santiago sets the plan after your consultation.
